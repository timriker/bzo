# Audio and voice

- `Settings -> Audio Settings` — game, voice, and microphone levels, each from
  Off to 100%
- **Voice channel** — **All** is everyone on the server, **Nearby** is only
  players within earshot, **Team** is your own team anywhere on the map
- `B` — toggle the microphone

Voice is available after microphone permission is granted. Observers talk and
listen on the same terms as everyone else.
