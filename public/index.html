HUD Buttons

  • F — Fullscreen (or click the Fullscreen button)
  • ` — Debug HUD (or click the Debug button)
  • M — Mouse Steering (or click the Mouse Steering button)
  • -/= or Numpad -/+ — Radar Zoom
  • \ — Radar Zoom Reset (Medium)
  • Radar: ... button in Settings — Cycle Short/Medium/Long presets
  • C — Camera View (or click the Camera button)
  • O — Operator Panel (or click the Operator button)
  • / or ? — Show/Hide Help (or click the Help button)

Movement & Actions

  • W/S or Up/Down — Move Forward/Backward
  • A/D or Left/Right — Turn Left/Right
  • Enter — Shoot
  • Tab — Jump
  • Space — Drop Flag
  • I — Identify: names the tank in your sights, and locks a Guided Missile onto it
  • Q — Self-Destruct: five seconds, and Q again calls it off
  • C — Toggle Camera View
  • P — Pause Game
  • O — Operator Panel

Flags

Drive over a flag to pick it up, and Space to drop it. A superflag on the ground is white and tells you nothing about itself until somebody picks it up.

Mobile On-Screen Controls

  • ⤒ — Jump (tap button)
  • ● — Fire (tap button)
  • ⚑ — Drop Flag (tap button)
  • ◎ — Identify (tap button)
  • Use on-screen joystick to move/turn

Mouse Steering

  • M or the Mouse Steering row — Enable/Disable
  • ESC — Leave mouse steering (see Dialogs & Menus)
  • Mouse Up/Down — Control Speed
  • Mouse Left/Right — Turn Tank
  • Targeting Box — Inside the small box the tank holds still; the outer box edge is full turn and full speed, and going further out adds nothing
  • The keys still drive: hold a key and it owns that axis, let go and the mouse has it back. Steer with the mouse while holding W, or turn with A/D while the mouse sets the speed.
  • Left Click — Shoot, anywhere on screen unless chat is active or the on-screen controls are up
  • Right Click — Identify: locks a Guided Missile onto the tank in your sights, and picks the roaming target while observing
  • The row is unavailable with no mouse attached, in VR, or while the on-screen controls are up, since those steer instead

Dialogs & Menus

  • ESC — Back out one step, and Settings is the last stop: leave chat, leave fullscreen, leave mouse steering, then open Settings. One more press closes it, so pressing ESC once too often costs nothing
  • Up/Down — Previous/next control, including from inside a text field
  • Left/Right — Change the focused control: move a slider, pick the next choice. In a text field they move the cursor instead
  • Tab / Shift+Tab — Previous/next control
  • Home / End — First/last control
  • Enter or Space — Press the focused control
  • This Help panel is read rather than operated, so here the arrows scroll it and only Left/Right move focus

Chat & Misc

  • N — Open Chat (or click the Send button)
  • Enter — Send Chat (or click the Send button again)
  • ESC — Exit Chat Input
  • 1/2/3/4/5 — Chat tabs: All/Chat/Server/Misc/Debug
  • [ / ] — Previous/Next chat tab
  • . — Reply to last direct-message sender
  • , — Message nemesis target
  • Page Up / Page Down — Chat scrollback
  • End — Jump chat to newest message
  • Click Player Name — Open Settings

Observer

  • Drive / Turn — Fly the camera; an Observer has no tank
  • Tab / Space — Climb / Descend
  • Enter or C — Step through views and players: leader, each player, next view
  • Views: Free, Track, Follow, Driving with, Flag
  • I or Right Click — In the Free view, watch the tank in your sights
  • Click a scoreboard row — Watch that player; click it again for the leader
  • The camera rests at the eye height of a tank on the ground.

Audio

  • Settings -> Audio Settings — Game, voice, and microphone levels, each from Off to 100%
  • Voice channel — All is everyone on the server, Nearby is only players within earshot, Team is your own team anywhere on the map
  • B — Toggle Microphone
  • Voice is available after microphone permission is granted.
  • Observers talk and listen on the same terms as everyone else.

VR & Gamepad

  • Either trigger — Shoot
  • Grip — Jump
  • A — Drop Flag
  • Either thumbstick press — Identify
  • B — VR menu
  • On a gamepad: either shoulder — Identify

3D Viewing Modes

  • Anaglyph 3D — Red/Cyan glasses for 3D effect
  • VR Mode — WebXR-compatible headsets
